# KSA Regulatory Framework Cross-Maps
_Last updated: 2026-08-08_

## NCA ECC (Essential Cybersecurity Controls)
- **Version:** 2.0
- **Controls:** 114 across 5 domains
- **Mandatory for:** Government entities, critical infrastructure
- **Domains:**
  1. Governance (1-1-1 to 1-5-3)
  2. Defense (2-1-1 to 2-7-4)
  3. Resilience (3-1-1 to 3-5-3)
  4. Third-Party (4-1-1 to 4-3-3)
  5. ICS / OT (5-1-1 to 5-4-3)
- **ISO 27001 Mapping:** 1:1 to Annex A controls + clauses §5–§7

### Key Cross-Maps (NCA ECC → ISO 27001)
| NCA ECC | ISO 27001 | Topic |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| 1-1-1 | §5.1 | Leadership commitment |
| 1-2-1 | §5.3 | Roles & responsibilities |
| 2-1-1 | A.5.1 | Information security policies |
| 2-3-1 | A.5.12 | Asset management |
| 2-5-1 | A.5.33 | Protection of records |
| 3-1-1 | A.5.29 | Information security during disruption |
| 4-1-1 | A.5.19 | Supplier relationships |
| 5-1-1 | A.5.24 | ICS specific controls |

## PDPL (Personal Data Protection Law)
- **Effective:** September 2024
- **Key Requirements:**
  - PIA (Privacy Impact Assessment) mandatory
  - DPO (Data Protection Officer) mandatory
  - 72-hour breach notification
  - Cross-border data transfer restrictions
  - Consent management requirements
  - Data subject rights (Art.12)

### Key Cross-Maps (PDPL → ISO 27701)
| PDPL Article | ISO 27701 / 27001 | Topic |
|--------------|-------------------|-------|
| Art.5 | A.5.34 | Lawful basis for processing |
| Art.12 | §7.3 | Data subject rights |
| Art.20 | A.5.24 | Breach notification |
| Art.11 | A.6.2 | Automated decision-making |
